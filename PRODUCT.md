# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers and design leads. They open the site to decide whether to hire Govinda for a product design role, especially one centered on AI agent UX.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio whose job is that hire decision. It shows the handoff Govinda designs: people decide, agents draft, so someone can trust what goes out. Success is a hiring reader who can see that claim and the proof behind it.

## Positioning

The claim is that handoff. The proof is three case studies still in progress: Manufacturing Intelligence, Nile 2.0, and Kynderconnect. They are not published on the site yet, so they cannot be presented as shipped work.

## Operating Context

The reader is evaluating a website. Govinda maintains it himself, locally, with `npm run dev` and `npm run build`. There is no confirmed public URL in the repo.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site already has a home page (hero, prototypes, shaders, 3D, contact), an internal prototype page (Ledgerline), NDLA case pages, labs (`/lab/glass`, `/lab/loop`), and a local flow canvas (`/flow`).

Govinda is a designer. The site must stay easy to run after `npm install` and `npm run build`, and first paint must not depend on secrets.

## Brand Commitments

The name on the site is Govinda.

## Evidence on Hand

Current public copy, in `index.html` and the home hero: “People decide. Agents draft.” and “I design that handoff — so someone can trust what goes out.” That sentence was not locked as a permanent line in this interview.

On the site now: Ledgerline (internal prototype), Waypoint and Grainhouse (placeholder links and placeholder covers), shader studies, a 3D section, and NDLA pages at `/work/ndla` and `/work/ndla-b2`.

Not on the site: the three agent-UX case studies. Manufacturing has a seeded source note at `portfolio/mfg/M0-source.md` and a planning checkpoint at `CHECKPOINT-portfolio-case-studies.md`. Nile 2.0 and Kynderconnect are named in that plan only.

No confirmed testimonials, customer quotes, hire metrics, or outcomes are in the repo. Do not fabricate them.
