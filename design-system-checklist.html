Reading the size columns. S Small / solo — one product, one team, one theme. Roughly 2–4 weeks of part-time work. M Mid product — several squads, light + dark, a real component library. 2–4 months. L Enterprise — multiple brands or products, versioning, governance, audits. 6–18 months and it never really "finishes".

Start here if you're a beginner. Do the S rows in Sections 1, 2 and 4 only. That gives you tokens, a type and colour scale, and about twelve components — which is a genuinely usable design system you can reuse on every project. Everything else is what you add when more people start depending on your work.
