## 1. Working thesis

**Manufacturing Intelligence** is an agentic ops system (workflow + BI + copilot) where the product moment is not “chat answers a question,” but **turning a data insight into a governed next action**—with human approval gates and a durable audit trail.

The portfolio story should prove: *I designed the trust layer between agent recommendation and operational action.*

### One-liner (case study hero)

From insight to action—without skipping approval or losing the audit.

### Expanded (hiring narrative)

Plant / ops leaders already have dashboards. What they lack is a safe path from signal → decision → execution. This product pairs agent-generated recommendations with explicit approval gates and auditability so teams can act faster **without** handing the plant to an opaque model.
