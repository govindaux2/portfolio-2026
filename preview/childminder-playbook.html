An AI app for a childminder’s day

This page is the plan, so the later app has something to be built from. It specifies routines, a live feed, memories, learnings, and AI assessments for a home childminder or a very small setting. AI suggests and drafts. A person confirms before anything is shared or treated as an assessment.

Setup, then a normal entry

One screen per routine. A sentence at the top (“Bottle feed. I need how much, who gave it, and a photo”). Under it, suggested fields the person accepts. Then a table: label, type, required, parent-visible, order. The example photo is labelled Example, so it stays out of the journal. On the day, pick children, fill the fields that are on, and save. Duration is calculated. Staff-only fields stay out of the parent payload.

What a saved feed stores

Name, who can open it, which routine types, which children, comments on or off. Staff notes stay out of every parent feed. Deleting a feed leaves the entries where they are. One item can sit in more than one feed.

Group memory

The feed item stays on the feed. The memory stores kind, tags, draft, approved parent text, photo ids, the consent snapshot at send time, and who sent it. Medicine and accident items stay care records. A journal note may say “an incident was recorded”, and the detail stays on the routine.

The gap worth building

Centre software already covers meals, naps, photos, comments, and a journal. A few of those products draft prose. Parent AI is built for a person logging their own child. A childminder logs other people’s children, so the confirm step has to be heavier. On the pages read for this pass, the open brief is a routine builder the childminder designs, a custom feed, a feed-to-memory step, multi-child memories with a preview per parent, and a signed assessment that waits for a person before it is sent.
