# Sources for the canvas-print research

Read on 12 November 2026. The page is `index.html` beside this note. Figures are copied from the pages below or calculated from them. Where a page did not give a price, the HTML says so and leaves it out of the totals, or marks an allowance as an estimate.

Nothing in the HTML is a quote from a Hyderabad printer to Govinda. Retail shelves (ArtzFolio, PrintPosters, OnlineCollageMaker, WallMantra, Khirki, Printo, Nestasia, Chumbak, Zepto) are what a buyer can already pay a website.

## How the two cash columns were built

Lean is a home-based sole prop: no new computer, ten 24×36 prints at OnlineCollageMaker’s public ₹2,999, metro plus a small petrol assumption. Scaled adds a 50 sq ft storage unit, a Croma-listed 16 GB laptop, an iPad at Apple’s list price, Adobe All Apps at a 1 Nov 2026 tracked pre-tax price, two trademark classes, and thirty pieces at the same public print prices. Helper wages were not found and are excluded.
