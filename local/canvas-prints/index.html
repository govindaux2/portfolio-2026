Govinda designs original art, pays a printer to put it on canvas, does a light check of the finish and the hanging hardware, and delivers framed or gallery-wrap prints to offices, hotels, restaurants, clinics, cafés, showrooms and co-working spaces in Hyderabad. He does not need a press on day one. The rupee figures below are government fees and public retail shelves as read on 12 November 2026, or arithmetic on those shelves. They are not quotes from a Hyderabad trade printer. Those quotes are the open item.
A print bought at the public retail price of a 24×36 (₹2,999 at one site) leaves little room under the prices those same sites charge the public. The business works if a printer’s trade price is lower than that shelf, or if the original and the visit justify a price at the top of the shelf. Both have to be tested with paper, not with hope.

Hyderabad buyers, from what is already on the ground

The verified local suppliers already sit in Ameerpet (Sri Raama Ads), Jubilee Hills (Imprima), KPHB (Wall In), Borabanda (Wall Arts Hyderabad) and Banjara Hills (Kalakriti, Iconart, and a PhotoStop virtual office). Printo has stores and says it has a factory in the city. A sales loop can start in those neighbourhoods, because the offices, hotels and cafés around them can already buy a print without Govinda.

National factories in Okhla and Noida, and framers in Mumbai and Bengaluru, ship across India. Paper Plane Design, Khirki, ArtzFolio, PrintPosters and 999store all describe pan-India delivery. A Hyderabad hotel can order from Delhi without meeting a local designer. The visit, a set that matches the rooms, and hanging the work are the parts a website does not do.

Kalakriti, Dhi Artspace and Iconart are the original-art relationship in the city. Their buyers are not the buyer of a ₹3,000 canvas. Wall In is the alternative when the wall itself can be printed and the tenant does not need to take the art away. Khirki, Jindd, RareMango and Paper Plane all tell designers to ask for a trade price, and none of them print the percent.

What a commercial buyer’s own pages ask for, when they ask for anything public: a GST invoice (Khirki and Jindd say they can raise one), room-wise or site-wise labelling (Paper Plane), and a size that matches a drawing rather than a catalogue (Khirki, Jindd, RareMango). Who signs the purchase order inside a given Hyderabad hotel is not on any of these pages.

What Govinda still has to collect

These were not on a public page. Until they are written down by the vendor, the cost totals use retail shelves and say so.

  1. A trade price, in writing, from Imprima, Sri Raama Ads and Printo in Hyderabad, and from PhotoStop, ArtzFolio, PrintPosters, Khirki and Paper Plane, for 12×18, 16×24, 18×24, 24×36, 30×40, 36×48 and 48×60.
  2. The same quote as gallery wrap, as a float frame, and as a rolled print. Stretcher depth (the public pages mention 0.75 inch and 1.5 inch) and whether hanging hardware is included.
  3. GSM, ink, and the varnish. Imprima names Breathing Color Timeless and does not price it. Camel picture varnish at ₹160 is for dried oil paint and is the wrong product unless the printer says otherwise.
  4. Lead time, and who pays if the canvas arrives damaged. ArtzFolio’s public rule is a free replacement if you report it within 3 days with photos. Get the Hyderabad printer’s rule in the same form.
  5. A packing bill from a Hyderabad stationery market, and a courier’s volumetric rate for a boxed 24×36 and a boxed 36×48. Per-500-gram courier floors are not that rate.
  6. A CA’s letter for monthly GST returns. The ₹1,500 in the scaled column is an allowance.
  7. The designer’s percent, if a designer brings the job. It is not published.
  8. Helper wages, visiting-card checkout price, Apple Pencil price, and a real stock-insurance premium. None of those were on a page that could be priced.

Suggestion, for an original that is delivered in Hyderabad, until a trade quote exists. Stay inside or just above that shelf. Do not go below the all-in public price of the same size, because the buyer can order that size himself.

  • 16×24, delivered: ₹2,500–₹3,500. The bottom is above ArtzFolio’s framed ₹2,045 and well above OnlineCollageMaker’s ₹1,499.
  • 24×36, delivered: ₹4,500–₹6,500. The worked example uses the middle, ₹5,500.
  • 36×48, delivered: ₹9,500–₹13,000. The worked example uses the middle, ₹11,250.
  • A whole hotel or a floor of offices: no public rate. Measure, then quote. Do not cut below the single-piece shelf to win the first lobby.

How to read the colours

Sourced A page named this rupee figure.

Calculated Arithmetic on a sourced rate and a stated assumption: 40 units, 4 litres, 10 kilometres, 2.36% of a suggested price.

Estimate No public price. The number is an allowance so the total is not silently missing a box of tape or a month of ads. It is not a vendor quote.

Not in totals The item is real and the price was not found, or the price that was found is the wrong product (oil varnish for an inkjet canvas, a per-500-gram courier floor for a framed canvas).
